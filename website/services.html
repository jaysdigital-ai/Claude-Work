FAQ

Before you ask.

Do I have to buy everything?

No. Plenty of clients start with one piece, like follow-up automation or a new landing page, and add more later.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. Your written proposal gives a clear timeline before anything starts.

Will I be able to make changes myself?

Yes. I build things so you can edit the simple stuff, and I'll show you how. For anything bigger, I'm a message away.

Do you write the copy too?

Yes. I write landing pages, emails and website copy in a plain, human voice that matches your business.

Which tools will you use?

Whatever fits your business. Most automation work happens in GoHighLevel, and websites are usually WordPress, Webflow or custom code.
