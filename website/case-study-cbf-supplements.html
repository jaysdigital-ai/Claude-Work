A supplement store on Shopify, built from scratch

Case study

CBF Supplements had no website, no logo and no brand look. I built all of it, and the new Shopify store was bringing in online orders within its first month.

The challenge

CBF Supplements is a sports nutrition store in Hobart that stocks a wide range of supplements from big-name brands. They needed an online store to reach customers beyond their shop and make it easy to buy online.

There was nothing to start from. No website, no logo and no brand look. Everything had to be built from the ground up.

02 / 03

My approach

  1. Brand first

    I created the logo and graphics so the store would have a strong, consistent look from day one.

  2. Organising a big catalogue

    With hundreds of products across many brands and categories, I planned the store so shoppers could find what they need in a few clicks.

  3. Front end and back end together

    I handled both the design customers see and the setup that runs the store behind the scenes.

03 / 03

What I built

A new logo and graphics

A bold brand look that fits the fitness and bodybuilding space.

A custom Shopify design

A clean storefront with home page sections for best sellers like pre-workouts, protein powders and fat burners.

Easy navigation

Products sorted by category (protein powders, pre-workouts, amino acids, health supplements and more) and by brand, with dropdown menus that make browsing simple.

The backend setup

Products, collections, payments and shipping settings, ready for customers to order from day one.

Shopper-friendly features

A free shipping bar that shows how much more customers need to spend, secure checkout with multiple payment options, and a newsletter signup for deals.

The results

CBF Supplements went from no website to a fully working Shopify store that takes orders.
