More from the site they already had

Case study

Sorell Mechanical & Hire liked Canva and didn't want to switch. I grew their one-page site into five, rewrote the copy for local search and fixed their booking form, all without leaving Canva.

The brief

Sorell Mechanical & Hire is a professional car mechanic in Sorell, Tasmania. Their whole website lived on a single Canva page. That gave customers too little to go on, and gave Google too little to rank.

They liked Canva and wanted to stay. The job was to get far more out of the platform they already had.

  • Redesign the site and rewrite the copy
  • Strengthen on-page and local SEO
  • Climb higher in Google Search
  • Get the booking and enquiry form working
02 / 03

The plan

Three decisions shaped the project.

  1. Stay in Canva

    Rather than migrate them to something new, I rebuilt the site inside the tool the team already knew.

  2. Write for search, then for people

    Each page's copy was built around what local customers type into Google. AI sped up the first drafts, and I then reworked every page by hand.

  3. Keep the client close

    We stayed in regular contact, and nothing went live until they were happy with both the copy and the design.

03 / 03

The work

Five pages, not one

Mechanical, storage, trailer hire and contact each got a page of their own, with space to explain the service and why to pick Sorell.

Copy that ranks and reads

Written to persuade customers and to target the keywords that matter to them.

Local SEO & Search Console

The key on-page and local SEO elements are in place, and the site is connected to Google Search Console so it gets crawled and indexed.

A form that works

The old embedded third-party form was the problem. Swapping it for Canva's built-in form fixed it and keeps everything in one place.

Offer graphics, on the house

I designed graphics for their offers to give the site extra polish, as a bonus at no additional cost.

The payoff

A bigger, better website that the team can keep up to date themselves.
