The story

From VA to the person who builds the system.

For six years I was the digital marketing VA behind an Australian business. Emails, landing pages, social posts, the website, the CRM. If it touched marketing, it crossed my desk.

That's where I learned what most businesses miss: getting a lead is the easy part. Keeping it warm until it books is where the money is. So I started building follow-up systems in GoHighLevel, and I haven't stopped.

The Digital Agila is that work, offered to businesses that want it done properly by someone who'll answer when they message.
