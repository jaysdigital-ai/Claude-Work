Why Agila

Wide vision, precise execution

Agila is the Filipino word for eagle, and our name is a nod to the Philippine eagle, one of the largest and most powerful eagles on earth.

An eagle doesn't chase everything that moves. It rises high enough to see the whole landscape, picks its moment, and commits with precision. That's how we approach digital marketing.

First we look at your entire customer journey: how people find you, what they see, what happens after they reply. Then we build each piece carefully and connect it all, so you can see exactly where every lead comes from and what every campaign is worth.

The founder

Strategist, builder and designer

The Digital Agila was founded by Jayson Perucho, a digital marketing specialist who helps businesses get more clients without adding more work to their week.

Too many businesses pour money into ads and good-looking websites that never convert. The missing piece is almost always the system behind them: the funnel, the follow-up, the CRM and the data. That's what The Digital Agila builds.

The work sits where strategy, technology and design meet: mapping the journey, building funnels and automations, designing the graphics, writing the emails and optimising for search. The result is marketing that behaves like a machine: predictable, measurable and ready to scale.

What we believe

Four principles behind every funnel, workflow and page we build.

Vision before action

We map the whole journey before building anything, so every piece has a reason to exist.

Conversion over vanity

Likes and impressions are nice. Leads, booked calls and revenue are the point.

Systems over hacks

Tactics fade. A well-built system keeps working long after launch day.

Clarity over jargon

Plain-English updates and reports, so you always know what's happening and why.
