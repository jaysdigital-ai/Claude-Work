Follow-up on autopilot

Case study

A business coach's funnel was bringing in leads, but every step after the opt-in was done by hand. I set up GoHighLevel to tag, email, follow up and sort every lead automatically.

01 / 03

Where leads went cold

This coach already had a funnel doing its job: leads were coming in. What happened next was all manual, so follow-ups slipped and warm leads cooled off.

She wanted GoHighLevel to carry the load: send the right emails, tag and organise every lead, and move them toward a sale on its own.

02 / 03

Mapping it out

Nothing got built until the whole lead journey was clear.

  1. Walk the funnel

    I went through her funnel end to end, to see exactly how leads arrived and what they experienced.

  2. Pick the moments that matter

    From the opt-in onward, I marked the points where automation would make the biggest difference.

  3. Sign off the plan

    She saw the full plan, what would happen and when, before anything was switched on.

03 / 03

The setup

Automatic tagging

Each lead is tagged by what they do, so she can see at a glance where they came from and how far along they are.

Emails that send themselves

The right email lands at the right moment, without anyone hitting send.

Follow-ups that don't forget

Anyone who doesn't act straight away is followed up automatically, so nobody slips through.

Lists that sort themselves

Smart lists group leads and keep themselves up to date, so every segment is easy to find and act on.

The outcome

Follow-up now happens on time, every time, and some leads have already become clients.
