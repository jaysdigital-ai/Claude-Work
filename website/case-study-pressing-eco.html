One website, two languages

Case study

A bilingual WordPress website for Pressing Eco, a family-run Swiss dry cleaning business. Customers see it in French or English automatically, and the client manages just one site.

01 / 03

The challenge

Pressing Eco runs a professional dry cleaning and laundry business, serving the La Côte region as a local, family-run company since 2006. Their old website was built on Wix and had become outdated. Their customers speak both French and English, so the new site had to work in both languages.

They wanted one website that shows in French or English, without building and managing two separate sites.

They also needed basic SEO so customers could find them on Google, plus help with their logo, graphics and copy.

02 / 03

My approach

I started with research and planning before touching any design.

  1. SEO analysis

    I reviewed how the old site was performing and which keywords their customers were searching for.

  2. Mockups first

    I created mockups of the new website so the client could approve the layout and look before the build.

  3. One site, two languages

    I planned the setup so both language versions live on the same website and stay easy to manage.

03 / 03

What I built

A new WordPress website

I moved them off Wix and turned the approved mockups into a clean, simple UI/UX that's easy for customers to use.

Automatic French and English

I integrated a plugin that translates the website into French and detects where each visitor is located, so they see the right language straight away.

On-page SEO

I optimised page titles, headings, meta descriptions and content so the site can be found on Google.

A new logo

I designed a logo that gives the business a professional, consistent look.

Images & performance

I compressed images and tested the site's speed so it loads well on phones and desktops.

Copy revisions

I worked through a few rounds of copy changes to make the content stronger for SEO.

The results

Exactly what they asked for: one simple website that speaks both of their customers' languages.
