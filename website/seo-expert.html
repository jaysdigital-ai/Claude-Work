On-page SEO

Pages Google understands, and people want to click.

As an on-page SEO expert, I go through your pages one by one and make them clear to Google and compelling in search results. It's often the quickest SEO win, because it improves pages you already have.

What I optimise

  • Keyword research and page targeting
  • Page titles and meta descriptions
  • Headings and on-page content
  • Internal links and image alt text
  • Structured data (schema)

Ideal for

  • Sites with little organic traffic
  • New websites that need a strong start
  • Pages that rank on page two
  • Businesses wanting long-term lead flow

Technical SEO

A fast, healthy site that Google can crawl easily.

Great content won't rank if Google struggles to read your site. I fix the technical side: speed, mobile experience, indexing, sitemaps and the errors that hold rankings back, then set up Google Search Console so you can see your progress.

What I check and fix

  • SEO audit
  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Mobile-friendliness
  • Indexing, sitemaps and redirects
  • Search Console and Analytics setup

What you get

  • A prioritised list of fixes
  • The fixes done for you
  • Monthly ranking reports
  • Plain-English updates

Questions people ask

What is on-page SEO?

On-page SEO is everything on your own pages that helps Google understand and rank them: page titles, meta descriptions, headings, content, internal links, image alt text, structured data and page speed. It's usually the fastest SEO work to show results.

How long does SEO take to work?

On-page fixes can start to show within weeks once Google recrawls your pages. Ranking for competitive keywords usually takes several months of steady work. I'll tell you honestly what to expect for your market before we start.

Do you do local SEO?

Yes. Local SEO helps you show up when people nearby search for what you do. That includes your Google Business Profile, location pages, local keywords and consistent business details across the web.

Do you work with businesses in Australia?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses in Australia and the Philippines. Calls are booked at a time that suits your time zone.
