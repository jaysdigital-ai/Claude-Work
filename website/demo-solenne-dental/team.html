The people behind your smile.

A small team on purpose. You'll see the same faces every visit, and they'll remember you.

Dr. Andrea Villanueva

Founder and lead dentist

Andrea opened Solenne in 2019 after years in a busy city practice where she felt patients were rushed. She wanted a clinic where people could ask questions and get real answers. She leads our everyday and cosmetic care.

If you understand what we're doing and why, the dental chair stops being scary.
General dentistryCosmetic bondingWhitening

Dr. Paolo Mendoza

Dentist, braces and aligners

Paolo joined in 2021 to build our orthodontic care. He looks after most of our braces and clear aligner patients and loves the moment someone sees their finished smile for the first time.

Straightening teeth is a long project. My job is to make every visit along the way easy.
BracesClear alignersRetainers

Bea Santos

Clinic manager

Bea runs the front desk, the schedule and pretty much everything in between. If you need to move a booking or ask about costs, she's the person to talk to.

I want every patient to feel expected, not processed.
BookingsPaymentsPatient care

Marco Reyes

Dental assistant

Marco assists our dentists chairside. He keeps instruments ready, explains aftercare, and has a knack for helping nervous patients relax.

A little conversation goes a long way when someone is anxious.
