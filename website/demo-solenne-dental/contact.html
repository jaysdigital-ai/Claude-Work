Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you accept walk-ins?

If we have a free chair, yes. Booking ahead is the surest way to get the time you want.

How much will my treatment cost?

We give you a written estimate after your check-up, before any treatment starts. Our price guide shows typical starting prices.

Can I pay in instalments?

For braces, aligners and implants, we can spread payments across your treatment. Ask Bea for the options.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Tell us when you book. We'll give you extra time, explain each step, and you can ask us to pause at any point.

Do you see children?

Yes. We see kids from their first tooth and keep early visits short and friendly.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A valid ID, any past X-rays or dental records, and a list of medicines you take.
