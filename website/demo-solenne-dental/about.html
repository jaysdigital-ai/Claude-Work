Named for the light.

Solenne is a calm, sunlit studio built around one idea: a dental visit should never feel rushed.

Dentistry can feel cold and rushed. We built Solenne to feel like the opposite: warm, quiet, and honest about what you need, and what you don't.

Dr. Andrea Villanueva, founder and lead dentist

Our story

How Solenne came together.

2019

Opening our doors

Dr. Andrea opens a two-chair studio on Liwanag Avenue with one promise: no rushed appointments.

2021

Straighter smiles

Dr. Paolo joins and starts our braces and clear aligner care.

2023

A calmer clinic

We redesign the studio with natural light, plants and a quieter waiting room.

2026

See it before we start

We add 3D scanning, so you can preview your new smile on screen before any treatment begins.

Where we are

Second floor, above the café on Liwanag Avenue.

Look for the navy window frames. Take the stairs or the lift, and Bea will be at the desk to welcome you.

What we believe

Three promises we keep.

Time for you

Longer appointments so we can listen, explain and never cut corners.

Honest advice

We recommend only what you need, and we're happy to tell you when something can wait.

Clear costs

A written estimate before treatment, and payment plans for bigger work.

Inside the studio

Light, quiet and close to home.

Beyond the chair

Part of the neighbourhood.

School smile days

Each term we visit local schools to teach brushing and flossing, and hand out toothbrush kits.

A greener studio

We cut single-use plastics where it's safe to and offer bamboo toothbrushes to every patient.

Always learning

Our dentists train every year in new techniques, from clear aligners to cosmetic bonding.
