Everyday care

Most of what we do is quiet, preventive work. Twice a year we check every tooth and your gums, clean away tartar, and fix small cavities before they grow. You leave with fresh teeth and a clear picture of what, if anything, needs attention.

  • Full check-up and gum assessment
  • Professional cleaning and polish
  • Tooth-coloured fillings
  • Fluoride and sealants for kids

How it works

From first visit to finished smile.

  1. Check-up

    A relaxed 45-minute first visit with X-rays and a full look at your teeth and gums.

  2. Your plan

Good to know

Questions about treatment

Do you accept walk-ins?

If we have a free chair, yes. Booking ahead is the surest way to get the time you want.

How much will my treatment cost?

We give you a written estimate after your check-up, before any treatment starts. Our price guide shows typical starting prices.

Can I pay in instalments?

For braces, aligners and implants, we can spread payments across your treatment. Ask Bea for the options.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Tell us when you book. We'll give you extra time, explain each step, and you can ask us to pause at any point.
