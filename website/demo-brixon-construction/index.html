01 — Company profile

A Cebu trading house for everything a job site runs on.

Brixon Construction Materials Trading distributes the materials and equipment needed for civil, architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire-protection works — including personal protective equipment and anything else a customer's project demands. Specialised construction activities are catered for too.

A sole proprietorship established in 2021 and owned by Ms. Jane D. Sample — an Accounting Technology graduate with an entrepreneurial mindset, who runs the business on integrity, credibility, commitment and competency, and above all is spiritually oriented.
