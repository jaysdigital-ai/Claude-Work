Questions people ask

What is an AI automation?

An AI automation is a workflow that does a routine job without someone doing each step by hand: replying to a new enquiry, following up, sending reminders or sorting leads. Rules handle the predictable steps, and AI is added where judgment helps, such as reading a customer's reply, drafting a response or summarising a conversation.

What does an AI workflow specialist do?

An AI workflow specialist looks at how work gets done in your business, picks the steps worth automating, then builds and tests the workflow. That includes the triggers, the messages, the timing, the points where a person takes over and the checks that keep it from going wrong.

Are these demos real working systems?

Yes. Each demo runs on a real database and real workflow logic, so what you click is what happens. The businesses and patients are made up, each visitor gets a private sandbox and messages are written to a log instead of being sent, so you can try everything safely.
