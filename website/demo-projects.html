Questions people ask

What are these demos?

They are projects we build ourselves to showcase what The Digital Agila can do. Each one is a working example, such as an interactive website or an AI automation, made for a made-up business so you can open it, click through it and picture how it would work for yours.

What is an AI automation?

An AI automation is a workflow that does a routine job without someone doing each step by hand: replying to a new enquiry, following up, sending reminders or sorting leads. Rules handle the predictable steps, and AI is added where judgment helps, such as reading a customer's reply, drafting a response or summarising a conversation.

Are these demos real working systems?

Yes. The automation demos run on a real database and real workflow logic, and each visitor gets a private sandbox where messages are written to a log instead of being sent. The website demos are complete, working pages. The businesses and people in them are made up, and the enquiry forms don't send anything.
