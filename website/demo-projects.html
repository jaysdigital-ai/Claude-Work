FAQ

About demos.

What's a demo site?

A sample website I build for a type of business to show what's possible, using made-up business details.

Can you make one for my business?

Yes. Book a free call and tell me about your business. If it's a good fit, I'll put together a demo direction for you.

Is the demo the same as the final site?

No. The demo shows the style and quality. Your real site uses your logo, photos, services and details.

Are these demos real working systems?

Yes. They're working builds you can click through, not static mock-ups. The businesses and details are made up and nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
