A plumber's brand, built from scratch

Case study

Ron is a licensed plumber who had no logo, no brand and no website. I gave his business all three, and the new site now brings in enquiries.

Services

01 / 03

The starting point

Ron is a licensed plumbing professional who was ready to grow. What he didn't have was a website, a logo or any kind of brand. New customers struggled to find him online, and had little to go on before deciding whether to call.

He needed the whole package: a logo, a set of brand colours and a website that turns visitors into leads.

02 / 03

How we worked

Ron handed me the creative reins, and that shaped the whole project.

  1. Straight to the build

    With Ron's full trust there was no need for a mockup round. I designed and built from start to finish in one go.

  2. Logo and colours first

    The brand came before any page, so the website had a strong, consistent identity from the moment it launched.

  3. Ron's final say

    Nothing went live until Ron had looked through everything and signed it off.

03 / 03

What Ron got

A logo that earns trust

Clean and professional, made for a local trade business people need to rely on. It took only a few small tweaks to land.

A colour palette

One set of colours shared by the logo and the website, so everything looks like it belongs together.

A website built on Wix

Simple to browse and clear about what Ron offers, with getting in touch made as easy as possible for anyone who needs a plumber.

What changed

Ron went from having no presence online to a business that looks established and gets enquiries.
