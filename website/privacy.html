Who we are

The Digital Agila (thedigitalagila.com) is owned and operated by JPerucho Digital Marketing Services, run by Jayson Perucho, a full-stack digital marketing specialist. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean JPerucho Digital Marketing Services.

For anything about your personal information, email hello@thedigitalagila.com.

What we collect

When you use the contact form

Your name, email address, business name and website (both optional), the services you're interested in, when you'd like to start and your message. We also record when the form was sent and the country it came from, based on your connection.

When you email us

Your email address, your message and anything you choose to include.

When you visit the site

Like any website, the services that deliver our pages receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't build profiles of visitors.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and send you a confirmation that we received it.
  • To prepare a proposal and, if we work together, to deliver the work.
  • To keep the website and contact form secure and free of spam.

We use your details because you asked us to get in touch (or to take steps before an agreement), and because we have a legitimate interest in running and protecting the website. We won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with the services that help run this website:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and processes contact form submissions.
  • Resend delivers the notification and confirmation emails.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts the inbox where inquiries are received.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs deliver the fonts and open-source code the pages use, so your browser connects to their servers when a page loads.

Some of these providers may process information outside your country. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. If we don't end up working together, we delete them within 24 months. If we do work together, we keep project and billing records for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. These rights come from the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and, where they apply to you, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR. Email hello@thedigitalagila.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines or the data protection authority where you live.

Security

The website runs over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and only Jayson Perucho has access to the inbox where inquiries arrive. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect yours.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
