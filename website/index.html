Questions people ask

What does The Digital Agila do?

We build the marketing systems a small business runs on: websites and sales funnels that win the lead, automation and CRM that follow it up, and SEO, email marketing and design that keep new customers coming. You can hire us for one service or the full set.

Do you work with businesses outside the Philippines?

Yes. All work is done remotely, and we work with clients worldwide. Calls are scheduled at a time that suits your time zone.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. After a free 30-minute call you get a written proposal with the scope, timeline and a fixed price, so you know the cost before any work starts.

Can you work with the website and tools I already have?

Usually, yes. We start by looking at what you already use and only suggest replacing something when it is holding your marketing back.

What happens on the free strategy call?

We look at your website, how leads come in and how they are followed up today, then agree on the first things worth fixing. You leave with clear next steps whether or not you work with us.
