The problem

Good leads go cold for three simple reasons.

Slow replies

An enquiry sits in an inbox overnight, and the lead books with whoever answered first.

No system

Leads live in spreadsheets, DMs and sticky notes, so nobody knows who to call next.

One and done

No reminders, no nurture emails, no second chance. The lead goes cold and stays cold.

How it works

Every lead gets a reply, a follow-up and a place in your pipeline.

I set this up in GoHighLevel so it runs on its own. You just show up to the calls.
