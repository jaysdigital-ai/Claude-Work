Helping Gumnuts get found on Google

Case study

A new WordPress website for Gumnuts, a Tasmanian day care and Outside School Hours Care centre. It's easy for busy parents to use and built to show up when local families search for childcare.

01 / 03

The challenge

Gumnuts offers day care and Outside School Hours Care to local families. Their old website wasn't doing them any favours. It didn't reflect who they were, and parents searching on Google had a hard time finding them.

They needed a fresh website that felt like their brand, was simple for busy parents to use, and showed up when families searched for childcare in their area.

02 / 03

My approach

Before designing a single page, I wanted to understand how local parents look for childcare.

  1. Keyword research

    I checked how the old site was doing on Google and found the exact phrases parents in the area type in when they need childcare.

  2. Design sign-off

    The centre saw the new layout and look as mockups and gave the green light before any building began.

  3. Pages parents need

    Busy parents want three answers: what care is offered, how to enrol and how to get in touch. I built the site structure around exactly that.

03 / 03

What I built

A fresh WordPress design

The approved mockups became a clean, modern site that looks and feels like Gumnuts.

Local search targeting

Every page title, heading, meta description and section of copy now speaks to the local childcare searches parents actually make.

Google Search Console

I linked the site to Search Console so Google can find and index every page.

Quick on any screen

I tested load times and fixed what was slowing it down, so parents get a smooth site on their phone or laptop.

The results

A website the centre is proud of, and families can now find it on Google.

  • 01A website the client loves

    They were happy with how easy it is to navigate and how well it reflects their brand.

  • 02Better visibility on Google

    Gumnuts now shows up first in Google's business results for searches like “Long Day Care School in Tasmania Australia”, so local families can find them when they're looking.

  • 03A smooth, stable website

    It has been running with no issues since launch.
