What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, keep small pieces of information on your device in the same way. This page explains what thedigitalagila.com, operated by JPerucho Digital Marketing Services, stores on your device.

What we use

NameSet byPurposeKept for
agila-cookie-noticeLocal storageThis websiteRemembers that you closed the cookie notice, so it doesn't keep appearing.Until you clear your browser data
__cf_bmCookie, may be setCloudflare (our host)Helps tell real visitors from automated bots, to keep the website and contact form safe.Up to 30 minutes

Both are strictly necessary for the website to work as intended, so they don't need your consent.

What we don't use

No analytics, advertising, social media or tracking cookies. We don't follow you around the web or build a profile of you.

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr and cdnjs. When they load, your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address, but they don't set cookies through our website.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and stored data in your browser settings. The website will still work, though the cookie notice may appear again.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll update this page and ask for your consent before setting them. Questions? Email hello@thedigitalagila.com.
