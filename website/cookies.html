What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, keep small pieces of information on your device in the same way. This page explains what thedigitalagila.com, operated by JPerucho Digital Marketing Services, stores on your device.

What we use

Both are strictly necessary for the website to work as intended, so they don't need your consent.

Analytics cookies (only if you allow them)

If you click Allow on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to see how visitors use the site: which pages are read, how people arrive and roughly where they're from. It helps us improve the website. We see totals and trends, not who you are. If you click No thanks, or ignore the banner, Google Analytics doesn't load and these cookies are never set.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy and may do so outside your country. Google Analytics doesn't store your IP address.

What we don't use

No advertising or social media cookies. We don't follow you around the web or build a profile of you.

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr and cdnjs. When they load, your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address, but they don't set cookies through our website.

Managing cookies

You can change your analytics choice at any time with the Change your cookie choice button above. You can also block or delete cookies and stored data in your browser settings. The website will still work, though the cookie banner may appear again.

Changes

If we add other non-essential cookies, we'll update this page and ask for your consent before setting them. Questions? Email hello@thedigitalagila.com.
