Australia and the Philippines

I'm based in the Philippines and work remotely with businesses in Australia and the Philippines. Everything happens online, so the distance never shows: you get one website designer and developer who answers your messages, books calls in your time zone and stays with you after launch.

Questions people ask

Do you design and develop the website yourself?

Yes. I'm both the website designer and the website developer, so the same person plans the pages, designs them, builds them and sets up the SEO. You have one point of contact from start to finish.

Which platforms do you build websites on?

I build on the platform that suits your business, including WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Canva and GoHighLevel. If you already have a site, I can usually improve it rather than start again.

Do you work with businesses in Australia?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses in Australia and the Philippines. Calls are booked at a time that suits your time zone.

Will my new website be set up for SEO?

Yes. Every site gets on-page SEO from day one: page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, fast loading and a mobile-first layout, so Google can understand and rank it.
