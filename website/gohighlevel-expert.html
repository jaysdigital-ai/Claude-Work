Questions people ask

What does a GoHighLevel specialist do?

A GoHighLevel specialist sets up and runs GHL for your business: the CRM and pipelines, the workflows that tag and follow up every lead, email and SMS sequences, booking reminders, forms, funnels and reporting. The goal is that no lead is forgotten and less of your week goes on admin.

How much GoHighLevel experience do you have?

I've worked in GoHighLevel for three years, building automations, pipelines and follow-up for businesses such as an Australian business coach whose lead nurturing now runs on its own.

Yes. I can review your current GHL setup, find where leads are slipping through and fix or rebuild the workflows, pipelines and automations that aren't working.
