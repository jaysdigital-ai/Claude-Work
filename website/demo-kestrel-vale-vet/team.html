Front desk and practice support

The first voices you hear on the phone and the friendly faces at reception.

Led by our practice manager Grace Whitfield, the front desk team handles bookings, insurance claims, reminders and the endless supply of treats. They are trained to recognise urgent symptoms over the phone, so if your pet needs to be seen quickly, they will make sure it happens.

  • Phone triageEvery call assessed for urgency by trained staff.
  • Insurance helpOn-the-spot claiming with most major insurers.
  • Friendly remindersTexts for vaccinations, check-ups and medication refills.
