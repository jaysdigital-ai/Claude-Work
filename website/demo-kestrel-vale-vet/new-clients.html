Your first appointment

A welcome visit is the best way to get to know us, and for us to get to know your pet.

Book online or by phone. We will text you a short new-patient form to complete before you arrive.

Check in at reception. Dogs on a lead, cats and small pets in a secure carrier, please.

A full health check, a weigh-in and a relaxed conversation about your pet's history and lifestyle.

What to bring

  • Previous vaccination and medical records
  • A list of current medications and doses
  • Your pet's usual food brand
  • Pet insurance policy details
  • A fresh stool sample if your pet has tummy troubles

Making it easier

  • Skip breakfast so treats work their magic
  • Leave the carrier out at home for a few days
  • Cover cat carriers with a towel
  • Bring a favourite blanket or toy
  • Ask us about calming medication

Frequently asked questions

Can't see your question? Call us on (02) 5550 4100 or send a message through our contact page.

Do I need to bring my pet's previous records?

It helps a lot. Bring any vaccination certificates and medical history you have, or give us the name of your previous clinic and we will request the records for you, free of charge.

How long is a standard appointment?

Our standard consult is 30 minutes. That gives us time for a thorough exam and to answer all of your questions. Longer appointments are available for complex cases or multiple pets.

Can I bring more than one pet to the same appointment?

Yes. Let us know when you book and we will schedule a longer appointment so each pet gets the attention they need.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payment plans through third-party providers are available for larger treatment costs.

Do you work with pet insurance?

Yes. We can lodge claims on the spot for many major insurers, so you only pay the gap. For other insurers we provide all the paperwork you need.

What if my pet is very anxious or aggressive?

Tell us when you book. We can schedule a quiet time, meet you in the car park, or prescribe pre-visit calming medication. We never force an anxious animal through an exam.

Do you see emergencies?

Yes, during opening hours. Call ahead on our emergency line so we can prepare. After hours, the same number diverts to our partner 24-hour emergency hospital.

Do you treat rabbits, guinea pigs and birds?

We do. Two of our vets have extra training in rabbit and bird medicine. Exotic patients wait and stay in a separate quiet area.

Worried your pet needs help right now?

During opening hours, call us and come straight in. We triage emergencies ahead of routine appointments. After hours, our line diverts to our partner 24-hour emergency centre, 12 minutes away.
