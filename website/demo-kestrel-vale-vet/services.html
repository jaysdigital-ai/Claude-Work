Wellness and vaccinations

Prevention is the most powerful medicine we have. Annual or six-monthly health checks let us catch problems early, when they are simpler and less costly to treat.

Every check includes a full physical exam, weight and body condition score, dental assessment, and a discussion of diet, behaviour and lifestyle. We tailor vaccinations to your pet's real risk, rather than vaccinating for everything every year.

What's included

  • Full nose-to-tail examination
  • C3 and C5 dog vaccinations
  • F3 and F4 cat vaccinations
  • Written health summary

Good to know

  • Annual check for adult pets
  • Six-monthly for seniors
  • Before travel or boarding
  • Any change in eating or energy

Dental care

Most dogs and cats show signs of dental disease by age three. Sore teeth are painful, but pets rarely stop eating, so problems often go unnoticed.

Our dental procedures are performed under general anaesthetic with full monitoring. We chart every tooth, take digital dental X-rays to see below the gum line, then clean, polish and treat. You will get before-and-after photos and a home-care plan.

What's included

  • Free nurse dental checks
  • Scale and polish
  • Digital dental X-rays
  • Extractions and pain relief

Signs to watch for

  • Bad breath
  • Red or bleeding gums
  • Chewing on one side
  • Pawing at the mouth
Dental procedure from $480Book this service

Surgery

From routine desexing to complex soft tissue procedures, our surgical team puts safety first at every stage.

Every anaesthetic patient has a pre-anaesthetic check, an IV line and fluids, continuous monitoring of heart rate, oxygen, blood pressure and temperature, and a dedicated nurse from induction until they wake. Pain relief is tailored to each patient and continued at home.

What's included

  • Dog and cat desexing
  • Lump removals and biopsies
  • Wound and abscess repair
  • Abdominal and orthopaedic surgery

Good to know

  • Text update when your pet wakes
  • Same-day discharge for most procedures
  • Free post-op check at 3 and 10 days
  • Nurse call the next morning
Cat desexing from $260Book this service

Diagnostics and imaging

When your pet is unwell, fast answers matter. Our in-house diagnostics mean less waiting and quicker treatment.

Our laboratory runs haematology, biochemistry, urinalysis and cytology on site, with most results ready during your visit. Digital radiography and ultrasound help us look inside without surgery, and we work with specialist radiologists for second opinions when needed.

What's included

  • In-house blood and urine testing
  • Digital X-ray
  • Abdominal and cardiac ultrasound
  • ECG and blood pressure

Good to know

  • Results explained on screen
  • Copies emailed to you
  • Referral partners for CT and MRI
  • Specialist report within 48 hours
Blood panel from $165Book this service

Puppy and kitten care

The first year sets the foundation for a long, healthy life. We guide you through every stage.

Our puppy and kitten visits cover vaccinations, parasite prevention, microchipping, nutrition and desexing advice. We also run small-group puppy preschool on Tuesday and Thursday evenings, led by our behaviour-trained nurses.

What's included

  • Vaccination course at 6–8, 10–12 and 14–16 weeks
  • Microchipping
  • Worming and flea prevention plan
  • Puppy preschool, four weekly sessions

Good to know

  • Socialisation tips
  • Toilet and crate training
  • Desexing timing advice
  • Free starter pack on first visit

Senior pet care

Pets age faster than we do. A year for your dog can be like five to seven years for a person.

From around eight years old we recommend six-monthly checks with blood and urine screening. Early detection of kidney disease, thyroid problems, diabetes and arthritis means more comfortable years together.

What's included

  • Senior health checks
  • Blood, urine and blood pressure screening
  • Arthritis and pain management
  • Cognitive decline support

Signs to watch for

  • Slowing down on walks
  • Drinking more water
  • Weight change
  • Confusion or restless nights

Nutrition and weight

More than 40% of Australian pets are overweight, which shortens lives and worsens arthritis.

Our nurses offer free weigh-in clinics and structured weight-loss programs, with a realistic food plan, monthly check-ins and progress charts. We also stock prescription diets for kidney, gut, skin and urinary conditions.

What's included

  • Free nurse weigh-ins
  • Weight-loss programs
  • Prescription diets
  • Raw and home-cooked diet advice

Good to know

  • Monthly progress tracking
  • Treat swaps that work
  • Exercise ideas for all ages
  • Food delivered to your door
Weigh-ins freeBook this service

Behaviour support

Behaviour problems are one of the biggest reasons pets are surrendered. Most can be improved with the right support.

Our vets assess for medical causes of behaviour change first, then build a plan with you. That may include training, environmental changes and, where appropriate, anti-anxiety medication. We can refer to veterinary behaviourists for complex cases.

What's included

  • Anxiety and noise phobia plans
  • Reactivity and aggression assessment
  • Inappropriate toileting in cats
  • Fireworks and storm preparation

Signs to watch for

  • Separation distress
  • Excessive barking
  • Destructive chewing
  • Hiding or not eating
Behaviour consult from $180Book this service

Parasite and tick prevention

Australia has some of the most dangerous parasites in the world, including the paralysis tick along the east coast.

We recommend a simple, year-round prevention plan matched to your pet and lifestyle. Our team will explain the options, from chews and spot-ons to the annual heartworm injection, and send reminders so doses are never missed.

What's included

  • Flea and intestinal worm control
  • Paralysis tick prevention
  • Annual heartworm injection
  • Tick emergency treatment

Signs to watch for

  • Wobbly back legs
  • Change in bark or meow
  • Coughing or gagging
  • Laboured breathing
Heartworm injection from $110Book this service

Microchipping and registration

Microchipping is required by law in NSW, and it is the best way to be reunited with a lost pet.

Microchipping takes seconds and is no more painful than a vaccination. We will scan to check the chip works and help you update your contact details on the state pet registry.

What's included

  • Microchip implant
  • Chip scanning and checks
  • Registry paperwork help
  • Lost pet scanning free

Good to know

  • Puppies and kittens
  • Newly adopted pets
  • Before travel
  • After moving house
Microchip from $65Book this service

Rabbits and birds

Rabbits, guinea pigs and birds have very different needs from cats and dogs. Our team includes vets with extra training in their care.

We offer health checks, rabbit vaccinations against calicivirus, dental assessments, desexing and husbandry advice. Our rabbit and bird patients are housed away from dogs and cats to keep stress low.

What's included

  • Rabbit calicivirus vaccination
  • Rabbit and guinea pig dental care
  • Bird health checks and wing clips
  • Desexing for rabbits

Signs to watch for

  • Not eating for 12 hours (rabbits)
  • Fluffed feathers
  • Drooling
  • Weight loss
Rabbit check from $95Book this service

End-of-life care

Saying goodbye is one of the hardest parts of loving an animal. We are here to help you through it with kindness.

Our vets offer quality-of-life consults to help you decide when the time is right, palliative care to keep your pet comfortable, and peaceful euthanasia in a quiet room or in your home. We can arrange private cremation and keepsakes such as paw prints.

What's included

  • Quality-of-life assessments
  • Palliative and hospice care
  • In-clinic or home euthanasia
  • Cremation and memorial options

Good to know

  • A private room and side entrance
  • No payment at the time
  • Follow-up support call
  • Grief resources for families
