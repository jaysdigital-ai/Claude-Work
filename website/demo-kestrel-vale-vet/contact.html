Finding us

We are on Wattlebird Lane, opposite Banksia Reserve, a two-minute drive from the Kestrel Vale shopping village.

Parking

Free on-site parking for 14 cars, including two accessible spaces right at the entrance.

Public transport

Bus routes stop 150 metres away on Banksia Road. Pets in carriers are welcome on most services.

Accessibility

Step-free entry, wide doorways and a ground-floor accessible bathroom.

Worried your pet needs help right now?

During opening hours, call us and come straight in. We triage emergencies ahead of routine appointments. After hours, our line diverts to our partner 24-hour emergency centre, 12 minutes away.
