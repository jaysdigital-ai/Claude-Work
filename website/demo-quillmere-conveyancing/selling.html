A sale startslong beforethe sign goes up.

Whether you’re deciding if it’s time to go to market, sprucing the place up for a better price or still looking for an agent, the first job is the same: get the contract right.

01

The contract

The very first step when you decide to sell is to have a sale contract drawn up. It sets out the legal terms and conditions, and it also needs to say what’s included, what’s excluded, and any special requirements.

A contract can be varied in a lot of ways, so talk your situation through with us. Look at your disclosure responsibilities too. Failing to disclose certain things can become a real headache later.

02

The deposit

The purchaser pays the deposit once the contract has been signed and is legally binding. It’s usually held in your selling agent’s trust account until settlement.

03

Before settlement

The settlement date depends on the conditions in the contract. In the meantime we coordinate with your bank and with the purchaser’s conveyancer.

Your part is simpler: arrange to disconnect the electricity and other services, and start packing.

04

Settlement

The purchase price is adjusted before settlement so that council rates, water rates and land tax are shared fairly between you and the buyer.

When settlement is done, the real estate agent releases the deposit to you, less their commission. And the board out the front gets turned around.
