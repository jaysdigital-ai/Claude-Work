A small officethat comes to you.

Quillmere Conveyancing started in 2016 as a mobile practice, because the people who most need a conveyancer are usually at work during office hours. There’s a shop in Lakeview now. The car still gets plenty of use.

Sarah Quillmere

Owner · Licensed conveyancer

Sarah has worked in the legal industry for over 14 years and completed her Advanced Diploma in Conveyancing in 2016. She’s passionate about property investing and development, and invested in her clients’ wellbeing.

She stays current on conveyancing procedure, legislation and the council planning schemes, and can tell you whether a property suits what you intend to do with it.

sarah@quillmereconveyancing.example

Emily Tarnwick

Conveyancing assistant · Mondays & Fridays, 9–5

Emily makes sure every file is running on schedule and is happy to help with any question. Since joining she has built up her knowledge of the industry and is taking on further training to make it her career.

She enjoys talking to clients and helping them get where they’re going.
