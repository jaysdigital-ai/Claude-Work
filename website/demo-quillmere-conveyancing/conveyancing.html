Home/How it works

One property.Quite a few layers.

Conveyancing is the transfer of real estate between a seller and a buyer. As simple as that sounds, it touches contracts, mortgages, caveats, easements, covenants and a good deal more. Here’s how the pieces fit.

01

The contract is drawn

The process starts as soon as the contract for sale has been drawn up. It should include:

  • The property’s title details and street address
  • The time between signing and completion
  • What’s included in the sale and what isn’t
  • Any conditions specific to this property

02

Both parties sign

Make sure you understand the exact terms and conditions before you sign. Withdrawing from the agreement afterwards is, most of the time, very difficult.

03

While it completes

Both sides have a long list to get through while the contract is on its way to completion. The important ones:

The buyer

  • Checks on the property
  • Organising insurance
  • Loan arrangements

The seller

  • Mortgage discharge
  • Signing the transfer
  • Agreeing adjustments

04

Settlement day

Everything has to be in order. The purchaser’s representative arrives with the funds. The vendor’s representative brings the title and the documents needed to release the mortgage.

Once everything is handed over, keys included, the property is settled.

05

After settlement

The change of ownership takes place: the new owner is registered on the title and the relevant government bodies are informed.

Decisions this large shouldn’t be faced alone, and you don’t have to go far for help.
