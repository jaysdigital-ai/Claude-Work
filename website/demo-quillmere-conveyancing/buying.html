Before you sign,walk up the pathwith us.

Buying a home is a huge decision and the process is complicated. Go through it correctly and you’ll be fine, which is why it pays to have someone beside you who knows property law and knows the area.

01

Signing the contract

This stage is straightforward, provided you do it in the right order. If you want to make an offer, take the contract the other party gives you and bring it to us before you sign.

We’ll go through your rights and your responsibilities in plain English, while you can still change your mind.

02

Finance

Go through this stage as carefully as you can. Read every document, including the pre-contractual statement that sets out the fees and charges you’ll be dealing with. Missed details here are the ones people regret.

You have the right to get legal advice, and you are never obliged to sign anything straight away.

03

Building inspections

Once the other party has accepted your offer, a building inspection is arranged. You get a written report on the condition of the property, and we help you work out what it means for the contract.

04

Settlement

Once the contract is binding, the work moves to us:

  • Arranging the stamp duty payment
  • Coordinating with your lender for the mortgage
  • Checking whether any government authority has an interest in the property
  • Dealing with outstanding debts, if there are any
  • Calculating adjustments for council rates and water
  • Doing final checks on the title

On the day, funds are handed over in exchange for the certificate of title and the keys. We attend the meeting for you.
