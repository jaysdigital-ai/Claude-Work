02

Duty & adjustments

Buyers pay transfer duty to the state. Both sides share council rates, water and land tax, apportioned to the day of settlement. We do those sums and show our working.

03

Searches & registration

Councils and government bodies charge for the certificates and searches a careful conveyance needs, and for registering the transfer. They’re itemised in your quote.
