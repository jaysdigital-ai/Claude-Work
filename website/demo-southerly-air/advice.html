Quick checks

Before you call us, try these.

Plenty of call-outs turn out to be one of these. If none of them help, we're a phone call away.

  1. 1

    Check the remote & mode

    Batteries, the mode (cool vs fan) and the set temperature.

  2. 2

    Check the circuit breaker

    Air con usually has its own breaker in the switchboard.

  3. 3

    Clean the filters

    Blocked filters are the number-one cause of weak airflow.

  4. 4

    Look at the outdoor unit

    Clear leaves, bins or plants away from it.

Energy-saving checklist

Cut your cooling bill this summer.

  • Set cooling to 24–25° and heating to 18–20°
  • Use zones to cool only rooms in use
  • Close curtains on west-facing windows by noon
  • Run ceiling fans with the air con to feel cooler
  • Service once a year to keep efficiency up
  • Turn it off when you leave, not to standby

Common questions

Asked every week.

Why is my air con blowing warm air?
Check it's in cool mode and the filters are clean. If it still blows warm, it may be low on refrigerant or have a faulty sensor, which needs a technician.
Why is water dripping from my indoor unit?
Usually a blocked drain line or a very dirty filter. Turn it off and book a service before it damages the wall.
Is it cheaper to leave the air con on all day?
Generally no. Turning it off when you're out, and using a timer to start it before you get home, uses less energy.
How long does an air conditioner last?
Ten to fifteen years with regular servicing. Coastal homes may be shorter because of salt air.

Too hot, too cold, or just not right?
