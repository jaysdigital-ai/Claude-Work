Questions

Good to know.

How quickly can you get to me?
For breakdowns we hold same-day slots across Sydney until about 1pm. After that it's usually first thing next morning. Care plan members get priority.
Do you give fixed prices?
Yes. Repairs are quoted on site before any work starts. New installs get a written quote after a free in-home assessment.
Which brands do you work with?
All the major reverse-cycle brands. We'll recommend what suits your home and budget rather than whatever we have in stock.
Is there a warranty?
Every install comes with our 5-year workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer's warranty, which is usually 5 to 7 years.
Do I need to be home?
For installs, yes at the start and end. For servicing, many customers leave a key or give access and we text photos when we're done.

Too hot, too cold, or just not right?

Tell us what's going on and we'll have a technician at your door, usually the same or next day. Upfront pricing before any work starts.
