Our story

One van, a lot of sweaty summers, and a promise.

Southerly started in 2009 when Daniel Reyes got tired of seeing customers left waiting for days. Seventeen years later, the promise is the same: show up, fix it properly, explain it plainly.

Meet the teamVisit the workshop
What we believe

Comfort is personal. So is service.

We're not the cheapest and we don't try to be. We're the team you call when you want it done right the first time, by someone who'll still pick up the phone in five years.

Every technician is employed by us, trained by us and drives a Southerly van. We don't sub out installs, and we don't pay commission on sales. That means you get the system you actually need.

Our values

Four things we won't compromise on.

On time

Two-hour arrival windows and a text when we're 20 minutes out.

Plain English

We explain what's wrong and what it'll cost before we start.

Respect your home

Shoe covers, drop sheets, and we vacuum up after ourselves.

Built to last

Correctly sized systems and proper refrigerant handling, every time.
