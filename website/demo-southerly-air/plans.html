Plan FAQs

How plans work.

Is there a lock-in contract?
No. Plans renew yearly and you can cancel anytime before your next visit.
Can I add a system later?
Yes. Just let us know and we'll adjust your plan at the next renewal, or move you to a bigger plan straight away.
Do plans cover parts?
Plans cover the service visits and give you discounts on repairs and parts. Total Care also removes call-out fees.
What if my system is old?
We'll still service it, and let you know honestly if repairs are no longer worth it.

Ready for summer?

Join a care plan before November and we'll book your spring tune-up first.
