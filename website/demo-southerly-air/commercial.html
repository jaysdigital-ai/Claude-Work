Commercial FAQs

The fine print.

Do you work after hours?
Yes. Most commercial maintenance is done before opening, after close or on weekends at no extra planning cost.
Can you take over from our current contractor?
Yes. We'll audit the site, take over the asset register and fill in any gaps in the service history.
Are you insured for commercial sites?
We carry $20M public liability (sample), workers compensation and provide SWMS and inductions for every site.
Do you provide compliance reporting?
We provide logbooks, photos and service records, including cooling tower reporting where it applies.
