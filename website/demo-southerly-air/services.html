Service questions

Before you book.

Split or ducted: which should I choose?
If you want to cool one to three rooms, splits are cheaper and quicker to install. For four or more rooms, or if you want a clean look with no wall units, ducted usually makes more sense. We'll talk you through both.
What size system do I need?
It depends on room size, ceiling height, insulation, windows and which way the room faces. We calculate it on site so you don't pay for a unit that's too big or struggles because it's too small.
How often should I service my system?
Once a year for most homes, ideally in spring. Twice a year if you run it heavily, have pets or allergies, or live close to the coast.
Do you remove my old unit?
Yes. We recover the refrigerant legally and dispose of the old unit responsibly, included in our install price.
Can you fix any brand?
We service and repair all major split and ducted brands, and carry common parts in every van.

Too hot, too cold, or just not right?

Tell us what's going on and we'll have a technician at your door, usually the same or next day. Upfront pricing before any work starts.
